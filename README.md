# Python Project: Unit of Measurement Converter

To practice Python, I created a simple unit of measurement converter for people to convert unit of measurement such as foot, inch, and yard.

First, you need to enter your choice of unit of measurement to convert from.

Then, you enter the unit of measurement you want to convert to.

Next, you enter the starting measurement in the unit you want to convert from.

In the end, Python does the calculation and returns the results.
